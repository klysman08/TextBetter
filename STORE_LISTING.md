## 📝 Detailed Description (For Chrome Web Store)

Transform your writing instantly anywhere on the web. **TextBetter** is a privacy-first, client-side AI writing assistant powered by Google's Gemini models. Whenever you highlight text in your browser, TextBetter lets you rewrite, fix grammar, adjust tone, translate into 15+ languages, and replace text with a single click.

Designed with **Shadcn Zinc** aesthetics, interactive sound design, and an intelligent keyboard shortcut engine, TextBetter brings desktop-grade AI productivity straight into your web workflow.

### 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture

- **No Middleman Servers**: Your Gemini API key is stored securely in your browser's local sandbox (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **Direct API Communication**: Every request is sent directly from your browser to Google's official Gemini endpoint (`https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com`).
- **Zero Tracking**: We do not collect, store, track, or sell any of your highlighted text or personal data.

---

### 💡 How to Get Started

1. Install TextBetter.
2. Open Extension Settings and enter your free Google Gemini API Key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/).
3. Highlight any text on any website and click the floating `✦` button or press your trigger shortcut (`Ctrl+Shift+K`).
4. Select your action and click **Replace** to overwrite the text or **Copy** to clipboard!
